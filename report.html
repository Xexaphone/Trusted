Trust & Safety Scoring Methodology

Complete transparency breakdown of how our client-side heuristic engine computes website safety ratings without paid APIs or personal data tracking.

Why Might a Score Occasionally Vary?

Because our add-on is 100% free and client-side, it relies on live public infrastructure lookups (such as domain registries, global traffic metrics, and archive databases) without running on paid centralized servers. If a public registry experiences temporary network lag or times out, a safe fallback state is applied, which can occasionally shift a score by a point. To ensure a seamless experience, scores are cached for your session once loaded.

The 10-Point Weighted Heuristic Model

Our extension evaluates websites across five core technical and reputational pillars, aggregating points into a final 1 to 10 scale:

Pillar Metric Evaluated Max Points
Protocol Active HTTPS encryption vs insecure HTTP. 2 pts
Domain Age RDAP registration or Wayback Machine active history (>3 yrs = 2pts, 1–3 yrs = 1pt). 2 pts
Archive History First indexed footprint on the Wayback Machine (>1 year established). 2 pts
Hosting Infrastructure Recognized enterprise cloud, CDN, or telecom ASN (e.g., Cloudflare, AWS, BBC, Google, etc.). 2 pts
User Traffic & Threats Tranco Global Traffic Rank and Quad9 community malware/phishing blocklist status. 2 pts
Safety Guards & Threat Caps

If a domain is actively flagged on global security threat feeds (such as Quad9 DNS blocklists), the algorithm automatically applies a strict safety cap, limiting the total score to a maximum of 2/10 and issuing an immediate warning banner.

Privacy & Zero-Key Architecture

All checks are performed client-side using direct, free public registries (RDAP, Google DNS, Tranco, Wayback Machine). No user browsing history is tracked, logged, or sent to external servers.
